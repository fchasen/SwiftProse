# Unicode

Latin with diacritics: café, naïve, Zoë, jalapeño, Ærø.

Combining marks: é (e + acute), à́ (two combiners), ȫ.

CJK: 日本語のテキスト、中文文本，한국어 텍스트.

Right to left: العربية and עברית in a paragraph.

Emoji: 🙂 👍 🎉 and skin tones 👍🏽 👋🏿.

ZWJ sequences: 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 👩🏽‍💻 🧑‍🚀 and a flag 🇯🇵.

Variation selectors: ☺️ ❤️ ✔️.

Surrogate pairs in maths: 𝕊𝕨𝕚𝕗𝕥 𝐏𝐫𝐨𝐬𝐞.

Invisible characters: a b (nbsp), c​d (zwsp), e‌f (zwnj), g‍h (zwj).

Curly quotes and dashes: “quoted”, ‘single’, em—dash, en–dash, ellipsis…

Tabs:	after a tab	and another.
